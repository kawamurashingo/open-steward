Maintenance needs a future.

Finding the next maintainer is only part of stewardship. Important infrastructure also needs enough time, review capacity, and testing to stay healthy.

WHY

Sustainable maintenance

For high-impact OSS, volunteer effort alone may not always provide enough continuity, compatibility testing, review, or emergency maintenance.

→
HOW

Fund capacity, not control

Open Steward is exploring grants, recurring sponsorship, fiscal hosting, and shared funding while keeping technical decisions with maintainers and the community.

→
CASE 01

DBD::ODBC sustainability discussion

The first concrete case: start with maintainer consent, understand real maintenance and compatibility needs, identify production users, then decide whether a small funding pilot would help.

Start small.

You don't need to adopt a whole project. One failure, one test, one investigation is enough to start.
